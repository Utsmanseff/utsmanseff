Why no map on the phone
An isometric map needs two hands and a large surface. On 390px it becomes a thing you fight rather than read, and the brief already says mobile is a document, not a narrow copy of the shell.
So the two ideas survive in translated form. The depth axis becomes a vertical year spine — the same information, read by scrolling instead of orbiting. The console becomes a sheet pulled up with the thumb, with every command as a 44px target. Same vocabulary, same log, no 3D.
768–1023px follows the document, per your earlier call. The map appears at 1024px and above, and never as the only way to reach a module.
Settled: the map is the landing view, with a skip link to the flat module list in the top bar. The console keeps a real text input, and the phone gets a one-line dismissible note pointing at the desktop view — worded so it reads as an invitation, not an apology, since the document below it is complete on its own.
